# Privacy Policy — ai-sync

**Last updated:** March 31, 2026

## Summary

ai-sync does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. It operates entirely locally on your machine.

## What ai-sync does

ai-sync creates and manages a `.ai-sync/` directory within your project folder. All files (HANDOFF.md, PROGRESS.md, PLAN.md, session logs, adapter files) are stored locally in your project directory. No data leaves your machine.

## Data collection

ai-sync collects **no data** of any kind:

- No analytics or telemetry
- No crash reports
- No usage tracking
- No personal information
- No cookies or identifiers
- No network requests

## Data storage

All data created by ai-sync is stored as plain Markdown and YAML files in your project's `.ai-sync/` directory. You have full control over these files — you can read, edit, or delete them at any time.

## Third-party services

ai-sync does not connect to any third-party services, APIs, or servers.

## Open source

ai-sync is fully open source under the MIT license. You can audit the complete source code at [github.com/oreolion/ai-sync-plugin](https://github.com/oreolion/ai-sync-plugin).

## Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue at [github.com/oreolion/ai-sync-plugin/issues](https://github.com/oreolion/ai-sync-plugin/issues).
